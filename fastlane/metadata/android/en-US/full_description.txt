<p>BilaiTube is a free and open-source YouTube client with a familiar YouTube-style interface in a warm amber colour scheme. Everything runs on your phone: there is no BilaiTube server, no ads and no tracking.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Home feed with YouTube's own category chips, endless scrolling and Shorts shelves</li>
<li>Smooth watch page: drag the video down into a floating mini player, throw it into any corner, flick it away to close</li>
<li>Swipe up for fullscreen, double-tap to skip 10 seconds</li>
<li>Quality, captions, playback speed from 0.25x to 4x, autoplay, loop, picture-in-picture and background play</li>
<li>Full-screen Shorts feed</li>
<li>Search with history, suggestions and mixed results</li>
<li>Download videos in the quality you choose or audio only; files go to Movies/BilaiTube and Music/BilaiTube</li>
<li>Subscriptions feed, history, Watch Later and playlists</li>
<li>Optional Google sign-in for your own feed, Watch Later, liked videos and playlists; the session stays encrypted on the phone</li>
<li>SponsorBlock skips sponsor segments, Return YouTube Dislike shows dislike counts</li>
<li>Light and dark themes, Warm Amber or Classic Red accent</li>
</ul>

<p>BilaiTube is based on PipePipe and NewPipe and is licensed under the GPL-3.0. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.</p>
