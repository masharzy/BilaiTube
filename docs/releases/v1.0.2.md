What's new in BilaiTube 1.0.2

- The mini player now stays on top of Search, channels, playlists, History and every other page; going back returns to the page you came from, not Home
- Going to Shorts pauses your video and brings it back as a mini player when you leave Shorts
- New "Background play" switch in You: when on, closing the floating player keeps the audio going
- Opening a video shows its thumbnail with a spinner until it starts, instead of the old video's frame or a paused 00:00
- The watch page shows placeholders while loading and fades the details in
- Swiping up to fullscreen (and down to leave it) now moves the video with your finger
- Liked, disliked and subscribed states are read from your YouTube account, and errors from YouTube are shown
- Channel pages show subscriber counts like 37.4M, and every video list shows length, views and upload time
- Watch Later reads YouTube's newer page layout and shows the reason when YouTube returns nothing

The in-app updater installs this over 1.0.1.
