What's new in BilaiTube 1.0.1

- Your YouTube account now fills History, Watch Later, Liked videos and the Subscriptions tab (your channels and their newest videos)
- Shorts: like and dislike show their state, double-tap to like, and a new Download button with the quality picker
- Leaving the app while a video plays turns it into a floating mini player; closing it stops playback, and nothing keeps running in the background otherwise
- Home refresh shows videos you haven't seen first
- Downloads: correct count, files deleted from your gallery disappear from the list, videos are sorted into folders by YouTube category
- Return YouTube Dislike is on by default, so dislike counts show
- Smooth animations when switching tabs and opening pages
- New design for "Open a YouTube link" with one-tap paste
- Adding comments and liking comments were removed because YouTube doesn't allow them from other apps

The in-app updater installs this over 1.0.0.
