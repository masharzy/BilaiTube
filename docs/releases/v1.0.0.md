First release of BilaiTube.

- YouTube-style home, Shorts, Subscriptions and You tabs in Warm Amber
- Watch page with spring-physics mini player, swipe-up fullscreen and double-tap seek
- Player settings: quality, captions, speed up to 4x, autoplay, loop, PiP, background play
- Search with history and mixed results
- Downloads in any quality or audio only, saved to Movies/BilaiTube and Music/BilaiTube
- Optional Google sign-in: your feed, Watch Later, Liked videos and playlists
- SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike
- Built-in updater

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK unless you know your phone needs another one.
If you used a test build before, uninstall it first.
